# Predictive and prescriptive analytics

The pages read the current Shopee order lines from Supabase `master_transactions`. The measures require eligible, nonduplicate rows. Revenue is **Deal price x quantity; excludes shipping, vouchers, platform fees; net of returned units**. Quantity is net units, and orders are distinct nonblank order IDs.

The latest transaction date does not prove that a boundary month is complete. A month is partial when coverage ends before its last calendar day, or when the first data month starts after its first day. Only an explicit row in `data_adjustments` changes that interpretation. Adjustment kinds are `no_sales`, `closure`, `data_gap`, and `anomaly`; `no_sales` and `closure` confirm zero-sales days, while data gaps have unknown demand and anomalies are real but unrepresentative sales. Any touched month is excluded from training and comparisons only when `exclude_from_training` is true. There is no percentage threshold. Adjustments do not change KPI or trend totals.

Forecasts cover revenue, distinct orders, or net units by province and product category. ARIMA(1,1,1) is estimated by conditional sum of squares; Prophet-style trend and yearly seasonality and a deterministic Random Forest are also available. Models use observed months; interior adjusted periods may be interpolated to preserve calendar spacing but are not scored. Missing months remain unknown and block a series. Holdouts use no months below six observations, up to six for 6–35 observations, and twelve for 36 or more. Reliability labels are `very_low` below 6 observed months, `low` below 18, and `backtested` or `backtested_weak` thereafter according to the last-month benchmark.

The training baseline is the latest 12 nonadjusted complete months. Forecast demand is the average across the selected horizon. Prescriptive year-over-year results align calendar months: each current month is compared with the same month one year earlier. At least six valid pairs with records in both years are required. Prescriptive scoring remains an exploratory relative sales signal, not a business recommendation.

The chart shows an adjusted-period band and explanatory note where an explicit adjustment affects its result. Comparison captions identify adjustments and reasons without inferring causes. Dataset bounds and adjustment warnings are returned from the analytics API.
